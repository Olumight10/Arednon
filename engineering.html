Engineering Services

At Nondera Energy Services, our Engineering Services division delivers comprehensive technical solutions designed for the challenging environments of offshore, onshore, LNG and pipeline oil & gas operations and solar energy.

We provide a comprehensive range of engineering, instrumentation, control, and electrical services that support both onshore and offshore projects.

Instrumentation and Control

Gas Analytics Equipment
Temperature Monitoring Instrumentation
Pressure Instrumentation
Level Sensors
Well Control and Communication

Metering and Calibration Services

Supply and Installation of Flow Meters (Coriolis, Turbine, Ultrasonic, Orifice, Magnetic, Positive Displacement, and Differential Pressure types)
Calibration and Verification of metering systems to maintain regulatory and fiscal accuracy.
Maintenance and Troubleshooting services to minimize downtime and enhance equipment lifespan.
Technical Consulting and Metering Audits to evaluate performance and compliance with local and international standards.

Telecommunications Services

Satellite communication solutions (for remote, offshore or pipeline sites)
UHF/VHF/Tetra radio networks for voice communication.
CCTV and video surveillance systems
Switches, VoIP, Internet solutions (data/voice integration)
Radio system infrastructure
Optic Fibre Cables installations
Flir Systems.

Solar Energy Services

Rooftop, ground mounted, and carport solar installations for residential, commercial, and industrial clients. This covers both off grid and grid tied systems.
Sourcing and supplying solar panels, inverters, batteries, charge controllers, mounting structures, and cables.
Maintenance and Troubleshooting services to minimize downtime and enhance equipment lifespan.
Routine inspections, cleaning, fault diagnosis, inverter servicing, battery replacement, and performance monitoring to keep systems running optimally.

Nondera Energy is your trusted partner for innovative, cost-effective oil, gas, and power services. We don't just meet expectations, we exceed them.
