Inspection Services

We offer all necessary instrument needed for Inspection works including calibrated instrument and loggers needed for air balancing HVAC, manometers, anemometers and sensors for measuring static pressure, dynamic pressure, temperatures, humidity and decibel. In collaboration with our international partners, we have the equipment and experience to execute test for determining air leakage rates and test the integrity of temporary refuge and LQ 's (TR-test).

We provide complete inspection services including:

Pre-Commissioning
Clean Room Testing
Environmental Tests
Temperature Mapping
Sound Readings
BMS/Controls
Balancing and Regulation
Commissioning and Testing
Electrical Testing
TR and LEV Testing
Rapid Response Shut Down Testing
Demonstrations
Pressure Regimes Airflow Direction Checks
Preparation of Detailed Reports

TR Integrity and PFEER Testing

To guarantee our clients' full compliance with SI 1995 No 743, the Offshore Installations (Prevention of Fire and Explosion, and Emergency Response) Regulations 1995, our specialists have extensive experience in the evaluation and testing of the porosity of TRs against the infiltration of smoke, fume and noxious gas. We will conduct the required testing according to COSHH procedures to meet the relevant regulations and each test is adapted to suit individual situations:

PFEER, Smoke & Gas Ingress, TR, TSR Integrity Testing
Thermal Imaging
Definitions of TR Boundary
Smoke testing
Unique Acoustic Service for noise control
Repairs to or replacement of modular doors
Pre-test surveys
Repair to or upgrades of HVAC systems and components
Testing and Verification
Develop and provide a maintenance plan to minimize future problems
Investigation of leakage paths
Hand over a full valid test report

Pipeline Tracking Solution

Nondera Energy Services in partnership with Petro IT UAE, delivers Pipetrak IT, a pipeline data management system that allows owners, operators and engineering firms to track, manage and report real-time data throughout the entire pipeline construction process.

Using the latest in web and mobile technologies, Pipetrak IT provides traceability of materials, inspection and construction quality, capturing data and documentation that assists in project management and meets all compliance reporting requirements.

Features:

Real-time Material Tracking (MTS) right from Pipe mill
Construction Management System (CMS)
Web GIS (Database) Visualization
Data Process Workflow
Project Completions Management
Electronic Document Database management
PipetrakMobile App for Field Data Capture for Android & iOS
