Operations Support Services

Procurement and Logistics

At Nondera Energy Services Limited, our Procurement and Logistics division serves as a critical link in the energy value chain, ensuring the timely delivery of high quality materials, equipment, and services that power our clients' operations.

Our team manages the full procurement cycle, from sourcing to delivery, covering onshore and offshore operations with precision and integrity. Through established relationships with reputable Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) and global supply chain partners, we provide clients access to high performance products while maintaining ethical and transparent business practices.

Manpower Supply Services

We recognize that people are at the heart of every successful energy project. Our manpower supply and technical assistance services are designed to provide clients with the right personnel, equipped with the right skills, at the right time. We understand that reliable manpower is critical to maintaining productivity, safety, and operational efficiency in both onshore and offshore environments.

Our manpower supply services cover the entire staffing lifecycle, from recruitment and selection to mobilization, welfare, and ongoing support. Through our extensive industry experience, we have built a network of qualified professionals across diverse disciplines within the oil and gas value chain.
