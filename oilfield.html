Oilfield Services

With a team of experienced engineers and strategic technical partners, Nondera Energy ensures seamless project delivery and measurable value for our clients across Nigeria's oil and gas landscape.

We provide a comprehensive range of services, including wellbore cleaning and pumping, liner hanger and accessories, cementing and casing operations, surface well testing, mud logging, and advanced drilling technologies.

Geophysical Services

Our Geophysical Services provide the critical subsurface insight needed for confident exploration and efficient field development.

Our capabilities cover seismic data acquisition, processing, and interpretation, as well as magnetic, gravity, electrical, and electromagnetic surveys tailored to specific project objectives. These solutions support exploration, reservoir characterization, well planning, and risk reduction.

Drilling Services

Our Drilling Services are designed to deliver safe, efficient, and cost effective well construction solutions that meet the highest industry standards.

Our expertise spans directional and horizontal drilling, mud engineering, cementing, casing and tubular running, wellbore cleanout, and completion support.

Well Services

Our team delivers precise well testing operations to measure flow rates, reservoir pressure, and fluid properties, enabling accurate reservoir assessment and production planning.

We provide integrated solutions that cover surface well testing, wellhead maintenance, and completion services, supporting clients through the critical stages of well evaluation, control, and performance optimization.
